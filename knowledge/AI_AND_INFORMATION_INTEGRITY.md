# AI and Information Integrity Knowledge

## AI-assisted journalism
Appropriate assistance may include:
- transcription;
- translation;
- document classification;
- summarization for internal use;
- query generation;
- data-cleaning suggestions;
- comparison of versions;
- workflow automation.

Human verification remains responsible for published facts.

## AI failure modes
Watch for:
- fabricated citations;
- hallucinated facts;
- hidden uncertainty;
- automation bias;
- model bias;
- privacy leakage;
- overconfident summaries;
- synthetic media.

## Information disorder
Separate:
- misinformation;
- disinformation;
- malinformation;
- satire/parody;
- legitimate disagreement.

Do not infer intent without evidence.

## Synthetic media
Verification sequence:

`provenance → earliest source → context → technical evidence → independent corroboration → publication decision`.

C2PA/Content Credentials can be useful provenance evidence when present and intact, but their absence does not prove falsity.

## Elections and public information
AI-related election reporting should examine:
- source provenance;
- platform governance;
- synthetic content;
- access to reliable electoral information;
- transparency;
- potential discriminatory effects;
- freedom-of-expression implications.

## Newsroom AI governance
Document:
- tool used;
- task;
- human reviewer;
- source verification;
- sensitive data exposure;
- publication disclosure when required.
