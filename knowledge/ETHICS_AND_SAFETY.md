# Ethics and Safety Knowledge

## Source protection
Before promising confidentiality, understand:
- the source's risk;
- jurisdiction;
- communications channel;
- what records identify the source;
- what metadata may reveal.

Do not expose a source through unnecessary detail in screenshots, filenames, document metadata or chronology.

## Vulnerable people
Apply heightened care with:
- minors;
- victims of violence;
- people facing retaliation;
- people whose identification creates disproportionate harm.

The public-interest fact often can be reported without publishing the identifying detail.

## Journalist threat model
Consider:
- physical risk;
- digital compromise;
- phishing;
- account takeover;
- doxxing;
- surveillance;
- legal intimidation;
- financial pressure.

For each risk, record mitigation and residual risk.

## Digital evidence safety
Preserve evidence without modifying originals. Use copies for analysis.

When authenticity matters, record hashes and provenance.

## Legal and ethical boundaries
This skill is not a substitute for jurisdiction-specific legal advice.

Do not:
- hack systems;
- obtain secrets;
- bypass access controls;
- impersonate people to gain unauthorized access;
- expose private information without a public-interest justification.

## Corrections and accountability
Keep an audit trail of significant changes and corrections.
